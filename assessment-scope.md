# Security Assessment Scope

## Purpose

The purpose of this security assessment is to evaluate ApexGuard Technologies' security posture before onboarding Meridian Financial Group as an enterprise customer.

The assessment will determine whether ApexGuard has appropriate security controls in place to protect Meridian Financial's information and whether identified risks are reduced to an acceptable residual risk level.

## Assessment Scope

The assessment will evaluate:

### Business and System Environment

- ApexGuard's business purpose and SaaS platform functionality
- How Meridian Financial will use the ApexGuard platform
- Systems and services that will process or store Meridian information
- The security boundary surrounding the production environment

### Data Protection

The assessment will evaluate how ApexGuard protects:

- User account information
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Insurance and claims-related information
- Limited financial information
- Other sensitive information introduced through customer workflows

Protection of information will be evaluated throughout its lifecycle, including transmission, processing, storage, retention, and deletion where applicable.

### Integrations

The assessment will include:

- Meridian enterprise SSO
- Meridian HR system integration
- ApexGuard APIs
- Connections between Meridian systems and the ApexGuard production environment

### Risk Management

The assessment will identify and evaluate:

- Existing security risks
- Potential risks introduced by the Meridian implementation
- Likelihood and impact of identified risks
- Existing mitigating controls
- Control gaps
- Residual risk following implementation of security controls

### Security Controls

Security controls will be reviewed across areas including:

- Identity and access management
- Authentication
- Encryption and data protection
- Vulnerability management
- Security monitoring and logging
- Incident response
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Third-party risk management
- Data retention
- Change and configuration management

### Control Effectiveness

The assessment will evaluate whether security controls are appropriately designed, implemented, and operating effectively.

Control claims will be validated using applicable evidence such as:

- Policies and procedures
- System configurations
- Audit reports
- Security logs
- Vulnerability scan results
- Penetration testing
- Business continuity and recovery testing
- Access reviews
- Third-party assessments
- Other relevant security evidence

## Expected Outcome

The assessment will determine whether identified security and compliance risks associated with Meridian Financial's use of ApexGuard are appropriately controlled and whether remaining residual risks require remediation, additional controls, or formal risk treatment before customer onboarding.

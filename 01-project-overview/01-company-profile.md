# ApexGuard Technologies — Company Profile

## Organization Overview

ApexGuard Technologies is a fictional U.S.-based B2B SaaS company providing insurance and risk-management technology to enterprise customers.

ApexGuard's platform enables organizations to manage insurance-related workflows, risk information, claims-related processes, and integrations with enterprise business systems.

## Business Model

ApexGuard operates a cloud-based SaaS platform used by enterprise customers across regulated and risk-sensitive industries.

Customers access the platform through web-based services and APIs and may integrate ApexGuard with internal identity, HR, and other enterprise systems.

## Customer Environment

ApexGuard is preparing to onboard Meridian Financial Group, a large U.S.-based financial-services organization.

Meridian plans to use ApexGuard to support insurance and risk-management workflows across its organization.

### Expected Use

- Approximately 420 Meridian users
- Three-year customer agreement
- U.S.-hosted production environment
- Enterprise SSO integration
- HR system integration
- ApexGuard API integration

## Data Types

The platform may process:

- Employee names
- Business email addresses
- Job titles
- Department information
- Insurance policy information
- Claims-related information
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Limited financial information

Payment card information is not expected to be processed as part of the proposed implementation.

## Security & Compliance Environment

ApexGuard maintains a security and compliance program incorporating:

- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework concepts
- Vulnerability Management
- Incident Response
- Business Continuity
- Third-Party Risk Management
- Access Control
- Encryption
- Security Monitoring

## Assessment Context

Meridian Financial Group requires ApexGuard to complete an enterprise security questionnaire before contract execution.

The assessment requires ApexGuard to demonstrate that its security and compliance claims are supported by appropriate documentation and evidence.

The assigned GRC Analyst is responsible for validating questionnaire responses, reviewing evidence, identifying control deficiencies and risks, coordinating remediation, and preparing a final risk recommendation.

## Disclaimer

ApexGuard Technologies and Meridian Financial Group are fictional organizations created solely for this cybersecurity GRC portfolio project. All systems, data, evidence, findings, and assessment activities are simulated.
